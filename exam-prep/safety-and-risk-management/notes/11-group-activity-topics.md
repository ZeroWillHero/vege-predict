# Group Activity Topics — mapped to your notes

The activity brief lists 9 topics. Each is an examinable sub-topic; this file tells you exactly where each is answered so you can revise them fast.

| Group | Topic | Where it's covered | Key points to be able to say |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | Importance of Hazard Management | [03](03-hazard-management-risk-assessment.md) §1 | Definition (systematic, structured, **proactive**); 5 employer benefits (R-I-B-L-E); benefits for employees and organisation; real-world success (nonwoven manufacturer $2M ROI) vs failure (Bhopal, Rana Plaza) |
| **2** | Workplace Inspections | [03](03-hazard-management-risk-assessment.md) §2.1 | Proactive hazard ID; 4-5 objectives; regular checks of operations/equipment/facilities; **worker involvement**; **customised checklists**; documentation; corrective actions + accountability |
| **3** | Exposure Monitoring | [03](03-hazard-management-risk-assessment.md) §2.3 + [05](05-physical-hazards-fire-toxicity.md) §5 | Definition; purpose (level **and duration** of exposure, decide if controls needed); hazardous substances, physical & biological agents; techniques by route — **inhalation** (air sampling pumps), **ingestion** (surface/hand wipes), **dermal** (patch/skin wipes) |
| **4** | Incident Investigation | [01](01-foundations.md) §9 + [03](03-hazard-management-risk-assessment.md) §2.2 | Purpose: sequence of events + **root causes** + corrective actions + prevent recurrence. 6-step (Secure→Gather→Analyse→Correct→Report→Monitor) or 7-step (Report→First aid→Secure→Collect→Interview→Analyse→Report) |
| **5** | Ergonomic Assessments | [03](03-hazard-management-risk-assessment.md) §2.4 + [09](09-past-paper-model-answers.md) Q1a | Definition; quantify risk factors → **MSDs**; 4-step process (injury data → walkthrough & feedback → objective tools → analyse/prioritise/mitigate); tools **WISHA, NIOSH Equation, REBA, RULA** |
| **6** | Qualitative Risk Assessment | [03](03-hazard-management-risk-assessment.md) §2.5 | Non-numerical severity × likelihood; prioritised risk list; **risk matrices**; High/Medium/Low; methods **FMEA, BIA, RAG ratings**; identify → classify → control → monitor |
| **7** | Quantitative Risk Assessment | [03](03-hazard-management-risk-assessment.md) §2.5 | Numerical values for likelihood and severity; **financial impact**; **quantitative FMEA (Occurrence, Severity, Detection)**, **quantitative BIA**, **Expected Monetary Value (EMV)** |
| **8** | Hazard Identification & Control Measures | [03](03-hazard-management-risk-assessment.md) §3 + [01](01-foundations.md) §5 | 5 identification methods (inspections, job descriptions, **JSA**, past incidents/near misses, **SDS** & equipment manuals); risk evaluation (likelihood factors + severity scales); risk matrix; **hierarchy of controls** |
| **9** | Acceptable Risk, ALARP & FTA | [03](03-hazard-management-risk-assessment.md) §4, §10 | Acceptable risk definition; **risk appetite vs risk tolerance**; legal/ethical/economic factors; 7 challenges; **ALARP** + grossly disproportionate test + 3 risk regions; **FTA** purpose, principles, construction, interpretation, cut sets |
