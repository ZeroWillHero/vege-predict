## Definitions to quote verbatim
- **Health (WHO):** "A state of complete physical, mental and social well-being and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity."
- **Hazard:** any **source** with the potential to cause damage, harm or adverse health effects.
- **Risk (ISO 31000):** "the effect of uncertainty on objectives." **(OHS): Risk = Likelihood × Severity.**
- **Safety culture:** the shared values, beliefs and attitudes that influence safety behaviour — "how we do things around here."
- **Toxicity:** the types of effects a substance can cause and its potency.
- **Acceptable risk:** the level of risk tolerated given benefits and the cost of further reduction; within risk appetite and tolerance.
- **Industrial hygiene:** anticipating, recognising, evaluating and controlling environmental factors/stresses causing sickness.

## Fire
**Triangle** = Fuel + Oxygen + Heat. **Tetrahedron** adds **Chemical Chain Reaction**.
**Explosion pentagon** = Fuel + Ignition + Oxygen + **Dispersion** + **Confinement**.
**Classes:** A ordinary combustibles (water/foam) · B flammable liquids/gases (CO₂, powder) · C **energized electrical** (CO₂, powder — never water) · D combustible metals (dry powder) · K cooking oils (wet chemical/saponification).

## Radiation
α = paper/skin, internal hazard · β = aluminium/plastic (Z<14 to avoid Bremsstrahlung) · γ & X-ray = lead/concrete.
**Deterministic** = threshold, severity ↑ with dose (erythema, ARS, cataracts). **Stochastic** = no threshold, probability ↑ with dose, severity independent (cancer).
**ALARA** = Time · Distance (inverse square: **2× distance = ¼ dose**) · Shielding.

## Noise numbers
WHO: pollution >65 dB · harmful >75 dB · painful >120 dB · night ≤30 dB.
NIOSH REL **85 dBA / 8 h TWA**; exchange rate **3 dB**; noise dose **≥100% = over limit**.
Raise voice at arm's length ≈ 85 dBA; must shout ≈ 95 dBA.
**TTS** temporary · **PTS** permanent (cochlea, irreversible) · **Tinnitus**.
Continuous noise = peaks ≤1 s apart; impact/impulse = peaks >1 s apart.
Hearing programme if ≥85 dBA: monitoring · **audiometry (baseline within 30 days, after 12 h quiet; annual at shift end)** · training · hearing protection.

## Temperature & pressure
**WBGT** = wet bulb + black globe + dry bulb (accounts for solar radiation, unlike heat index).
**Wind chill** = air temp + wind speed; defined ≤50 °F and >3 mph.
Heat: cramps → exhaustion → **heat stroke**. Cold: hypothermia · frostbite · trench foot · chilblains.
Pressure: **MAWP** = Maximum Allowable Working Pressure.

## Law & standards
**ILO** C155 (1981, national OSH policy) · C187 (2006, prevention culture) · C081 (1947, labour inspection) · C161 (1985, occupational health services). **June 2022** — safe & healthy working environment = 5th fundamental right.

**Economics:** **€2.2 return per €1** invested; **indirect costs 2–4× direct** (up to 20×).

## ALARP vs ALARA
ALARP = **P**racticable, general risk, grossly disproportionate test, 3 regions (intolerable / ALARP / broadly acceptable).
ALARA = **A**chievable, radiation only, Time-Distance-Shielding.

## FTA
Top-down, deductive · Boolean **AND/OR/INHIBIT** gates · starts at undesired **top event** · **minimal cut sets** (cause) / **minimal path sets** (prevent) · AND → multiply probabilities, OR → add.

## Ergonomic tools
**WISHA Checklist · NIOSH Lifting Equation · REBA (whole body) · RULA (upper limb)** → prevent **MSDs**.

## Qualitative vs Quantitative
Qualitative: non-numerical, risk matrix, FMEA, BIA, **RAG** ratings, High/Medium/Low.
Quantitative: numbers, **quantitative FMEA (Occurrence × Severity × Detection)**, quantitative BIA, **EMV**.

## Indicators
**Lagging** = past (injuries, LTIR, comp claims) · **Leading** = preventive (training sessions, audits, participation).
